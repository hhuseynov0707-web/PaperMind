2. What the Service does

PaperMind is a research tool. It indexes abstracts and metadata of scientific papers from public sources — arXiv, Crossref, DOAJ and OpenAlex — and lets you search them by meaning and ask questions answered from those abstracts, with citations you can open and verify.

We do not host or redistribute full paper texts. Links point to the publisher or preprint server, and access to full text is governed by whoever publishes it.

5. What the answers are, and are not

Answers are generated by a language model from the abstracts our system retrieved. Every citation shown is checked against the retrieved evidence before the answer is displayed, and citations the model invents are removed. This reduces error; it does not eliminate it.

Answers may still be incomplete, outdated or wrong. The Service works from an indexed subset of the literature, not the whole of it. Nothing here is medical, legal, financial or professional advice. Verify anything that matters by opening the cited source. You remain responsible for how you use the output, including any academic integrity rules that apply to you.
